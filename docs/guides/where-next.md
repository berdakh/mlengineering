# Where next

After Week 12 you have a finished project, a final assessment and a log of what you found hard. Choose the next
step by what your evidence still lacks:

| If the gap is | Spend the next weeks on |
|---|---|
| Depth on the project | The [advanced track](../advanced/): research-grade evaluation, a neural comparison, an authorisation-gated cloud deployment, a reliability drill and a clean-clone reproduction audit |
| Real operating experience | One small deployment that stays up, on your own cloud account with a budget alarm and a weekly review (read the [cloud guide](../tools/cloud-aws.html) first; it costs money) |
| Breadth beyond sensors and churn | A second project in a different domain (text, ranking or a public tabular dataset), built with the same rigour |
| Evidence others can inspect | Contributions to an open-source ML or MLOps project: tests, docs, a bug fix with a reproduction |
| Interview performance | Weekly timed practice: one coding, one SQL, one system design, one project walkthrough ([interview practice](interview-practice.html)) |
| Theory behind the practice | The [handbook](../handbook/) chapters that match your weakest week |

A course shows what you can do under known conditions; it does not guarantee a job. Describe your portfolio
honestly: say what you built, how you checked it, and what its limits are.
