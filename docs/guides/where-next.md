# Where next

Notebook 48 compares your Week 8 evidence with Week 1, and the Week 8 review day turns that into a plan.
Four directions, chosen by what your evidence still lacks:

| If the gap is | Spend the next eight weeks on |
|---|---|
| Real operating experience | One deployment that stays up: the `flowguard` service on a real cloud account with a budget, alerts and a weekly review |
| Breadth beyond time series | A second project in an unfamiliar domain (tabular business data, ranking, or text), built with the same rigour |
| Evidence others can inspect | Contributions to an open-source ML or MLOps project: tests, docs, a bug fix with a reproduction |
| Interview performance | Weekly timed loops: one coding, one SQL, one system design, one project walkthrough, each self-scored with the course rubrics |

Keep the skills ledger honest: *demonstrated* means an artifact produced under known conditions and reviewed.
An eight-week course shows what you can do; it does not guarantee a job, and the portfolio should not claim more
than the artifacts show.
