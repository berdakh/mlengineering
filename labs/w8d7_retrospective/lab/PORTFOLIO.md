# Evidence portfolio

One row per piece of evidence. The checks require: every one of the ten target capabilities (see
`ROADMAP.md`, "What the course is for") appears at least once in the Capability column, by number
(`1` ... `10`); every Artifact is a path in backticks that exists (relative to the course repository
root or to `WB_PROJECT_DIR`), or the word `none` when the capability is not yet demonstrated; every
row states Conditions. Conditions vocabulary: `independent`, `timed`, `guided`, `reviewed`,
`self-reported`, `not demonstrated`.
