# Skills ledger

A template for you to keep honest evidence of what you can do. Three categories are kept strictly apart.
Nothing here is a proficiency score.

* **Demonstrated** - there is an artifact produced under known conditions that has been reviewed.
* **Self-reported** - stated by you; plausible, not observed in this course.
* **Unassessed** - no evidence either way yet.

How to fill it in: list your prior experience under *Self-reported* before you start; move an area to
*Demonstrated* only when a reviewed artifact supports it (first: the diagnostic, notebooks 01-02); log every
piece of evidence in the table at the bottom with the conditions it was produced under.
